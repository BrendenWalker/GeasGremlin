Play-loop work resumes at [docs/plans/TRACKER.md](../docs/plans/TRACKER.md).

Status words are `todo`, `doing`, `done`, and `cut`.

- Set an item to `doing` before changing code for it.
- When you stop, leave a one-line note of what is true in the tree, not what you meant to do.
- The tracker's cut list stays cut unless the task explicitly reopens an item.
